WHO WE ARE

About SSRL

A Christ-centred ministry committed to sincere seeking, spiritual formation and revival labour.

OUR BURDEN

To see Christ formed in His people.

We believe genuine revival begins with sincere seekers — people willing to return to the place of prayer, submit to the Word and allow the Holy Spirit to transform their lives.

Sincere Seekers Revival Labour creates spaces for believers to encounter Christ, grow in spiritual understanding, receive practical formation and become faithful labourers in God's harvest.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our values.

Christ-Centred

Jesus remains the focus, foundation and destination of our fellowship.

Word & Prayer

We give ourselves to Scripture and prayer as essential disciplines of spiritual life.

Kingdom Labour

We pursue transformation that produces faithful service and kingdom impact.

Revival

We long for genuine awakening that begins within and reaches our generation.

“We are seeking not merely a crowd, but a people who will sincerely seek the Lord and faithfully carry His burden.”
